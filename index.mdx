## From invitation to incorporation

<Steps>
	<Step title="Invite your customer">
		Create a referral from your backend with a stable external reference. Expedite sends the founder an invitation
		to get started.
	</Step>
	<Step title="The founder completes their application">
		The founder signs in, completes the incorporation forms, and signs their documents. At submission, they can
		optionally share eligible incorporation documents with your organization.
	</Step>
	<Step title="Stay up to date">
		Follow formation and EIN progress from your dashboard or API. Signed webhooks tell your server when to fetch the
		latest referral state.
	</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
	Partner access is enabled by Expedite. Ask your Expedite contact for your API environment and credentials before
	making requests. The reference shows the production API address; access depends on your partner's rollout. Run
	examples from your own server or API client.
</Note>
