---
title: "Progress and documents"
---

Track the incorporation independently from its EIN. Read the referral whenever your user opens it, and use webhooks to learn when a published milestone changes.

### Read the milestones

Tracking begins when the intended founder accepts the disclosed referral. Until then, the API exposes your invitation receipt and `progress` remains `null`.

| Field                 | Meaning                                     |
| --------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| `invitation.status`   | Invitation delivery and acceptance          |
| `progress.formation`  | Incorporation progress                      |
| `progress.ein`        | Federal tax ID progress                     |
| `progress.nextActor`  | `FOUNDER`, `EXPEDITE`, `PARTNER`, or `NONE` |
| `progress.nextAction` | The next action to display to your user     |

Formation moves through `IN_PROGRESS`, `SUBMITTED`, `FILING`, and `INCORPORATED`; `ACTION_REQUIRED` and `UNAVAILABLE` describe conditions needing attention. EIN is independently `NOT_REQUESTED`, `PENDING`, or `ISSUED`.

`submittedAt` records founder submission. `FILING` and `filingOrderedAt` mean the filing-order handoff was delivered, not that Delaware approved it. `incorporatedAt` records incorporation approval. `einIssuedAt` records verification of the EIN evidence.

### Follow versions

Compare `version` when processing events and fetch the current snapshot. An event can arrive after a newer version is available.

`workflowSteps` and shared documents are evaluated on read. They can change without a new published version or webhook. Refresh the detail when a user opens the referral.

### Retrieve shared documents

At final submission, the founder may separately share their reviewed formation certificate and verified IRS EIN notice. Your client also needs `incorporation_documents:read`.

1. Call `GET /incorporations/{referralId}/documents`.
2. Select a `FORMATION_CERTIFICATE` or `EIN_NOTICE` from the returned list.
3. Call `POST /incorporations/{referralId}/documents/{documentId}/download`.
4. Use the returned URL within 60 seconds.

Internal notes, identity documents, SS-4 applications, and unrelated company files are excluded. The official shared document can contain an EIN or signatures.

### Respect changes in access

Withdrawing sharing stops new document access; disclosed progress tracking continues. A previously issued URL may work until its 60-second expiry, and revocation cannot erase copies you already retained. Suspended partners and canceled referrals lose eligible reads and notifications.

Expedite registers any referring-bank return link. The link does not assert that a bank account is approved.
