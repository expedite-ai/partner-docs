---
title: "Incorporation updated"
description: "A referral's published invitation or progress version changed."
openapi: "openapi.json webhook incorporation.updated"
---

Verify the signature and fetch the current authorized referral. When
`progress.ein` becomes `ISSUED`, the EIN milestone is ready. The event contains no
EIN number or documents.

See [EIN notifications](/guides/ein-notifications) and
[signature verification and delivery recovery](/guides/webhooks).
